# SuperBright

## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Product:** SuperBright
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Draft
**Target Market:** Indonesia
**Primary Language:** Bahasa Indonesia
**Secondary Language:** English
**Product Type:** Online Learning, Skills Development & Career Platform
**Product Model:** B2C + B2B + B2G + Education
**Inspired By:** Modern global learning platforms such as Coursera, Udemy, LinkedIn Learning, and Duolingo — adapted specifically for Indonesia.

---

# 1. Executive Summary

## 1.1 Product Vision

**SuperBright is an Indonesia-first platform that helps people learn practical skills, understand Indonesian society and culture, build careers, and connect learning directly to real-world opportunities.**

The platform should answer:

> **"Saya mau berkembang. Skill apa yang harus saya pelajari, bagaimana cara belajarnya, dan bagaimana skill ini bisa saya gunakan di kehidupan atau pekerjaan nyata?"**

SuperBright should not simply provide online courses.

It should connect:

**Learning → Skills → Projects → Certification → Portfolio → Career → Community → Opportunity**

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Indonesia has a large and diverse population with significantly different:

* Educational backgrounds
* Income levels
* Digital literacy
* Access to quality education
* Regional cultures
* Languages
* Career opportunities
* Workplace environments
* Technology adoption
* Government and regulatory environments

Many online learning platforms are designed around global or Western assumptions.

For example, a generic course may teach:

> "How to become a Product Manager"

But an Indonesian learner may need to understand:

* How Indonesian companies actually operate
* How decisions are made in local organizations
* How to communicate with Indonesian managers
* How hierarchy affects meetings
* How to work with government institutions
* Indonesian employment practices
* Local regulations
* Local consumer behavior
* Bahasa Indonesia communication
* Local business cases
* Indonesian salary expectations
* Indonesian technology ecosystems

SuperBright addresses this contextual gap.

# 61. Competitive Positioning

SuperBright should not attempt to become:

> "Coursera but Indonesian."

Instead:

> **"The learning platform built around how Indonesians actually learn, work, build businesses, and develop careers."**

The platform can borrow proven mechanics from global platforms—structured courses, credentials, projects, career paths and institutional learning—while creating an Indonesia-specific contextual layer. Coursera itself currently emphasizes career pathways, job-aligned certificates, projects, and institutional programs.

# 63. MVP Success Criteria

The SuperBright MVP is successful when it demonstrates three things:

1. **People want to learn on SuperBright.**
2. **People actually finish and apply what they learn.**
3. **Users are willing to return and/or pay for the product.**

The first MVP should prioritize **learning outcomes and retention over total registered users**.

---

## 63.1 MVP Measurement Period

Initial evaluation period:

**First 90 days after public MVP launch**

Primary cohort:

* Indonesian users
* New users who register during the MVP period
* Focus on learners aged 18+
* Initial focus on technology, career, business, and Indonesia-context courses

Metrics should be measured by cohort rather than only cumulative totals.

---

# 63.2 North Star Metric

## Successful Learners

A **Successful Learner** is a user who:

1. Enrolls in a course or learning path
2. Completes at least 70% of the learning material
3. Passes the relevant assessment
4. Completes at least one practical exercise/project

### MVP Target

**≥ 30% of activated learners become Successful Learners within 90 days.**

This is more meaningful than measuring course enrollment alone.

---

# 63.3 Acquisition

### Registration

Target:

**≥ 5,000 registered users**

within the first 90 days.

### Activation

An activated learner is someone who:

* Completes onboarding
* Selects a learning goal
* Starts at least one lesson

Target:

**≥ 50% of registered users become activated learners.**

Therefore:

5,000 registrations → at least 2,500 activated learners.

---

# 63.4 Learning Engagement

### First Lesson

Target:

**≥ 70% of activated users start their first lesson within 24 hours.**

### First Week Engagement

Target:

**≥ 50% of activated users return during the first 7 days.**

### Weekly Active Learners

Target:

**≥ 1,000 WAU** by the end of the MVP period.

### Learning Sessions

Target:

**≥ 3 learning sessions per active learner per week.**

A session means meaningful learning activity, not simply opening the website.

---

# 63.5 Course Completion

For courses under approximately 10 hours:

Target:

**≥ 40% completion rate.**

For longer learning paths:

Target:

**≥ 30% completion rate.**

Completion should mean the learner reaches the defined completion threshold, not merely watches the first few lessons.

---

# 63.6 Assessment Success

Target:

**≥ 70% of learners who attempt a final assessment achieve the passing score.**

If the pass rate is extremely high, SuperBright should review whether assessments are sufficiently rigorous.

If the pass rate is very low, SuperBright should investigate:

* Course difficulty
* Content quality
* Assessment design
* Learner preparation
* Instructor quality

---

# 63.7 Practical Project Completion

For courses containing projects:

Target:

**≥ 40% of enrolled learners submit the final project.**

Target:

**≥ 70% of submitted projects meet the minimum quality threshold.**

The goal is to validate that SuperBright can move learners from:

**watching → practicing → building**

rather than simply consuming videos.

---

# 63.8 Learning Outcome

After completing a course, users should be asked:

> "Seberapa yakin Anda dapat menggunakan skill ini dalam situasi nyata?"

Measured on a 1–5 scale.

Target:

**Average post-course confidence ≥ 4.0/5**

Additionally, selected courses should include pre/post skill assessments.

Target:

**≥ 20% median improvement in assessment performance**

between the initial diagnostic and final assessment.

---

# 63.9 Indonesian Context Validation

This is a critical SuperBright-specific metric.

After completing an Indonesia-context course, ask:

> "Apakah contoh, kasus, bahasa, dan konteks Indonesia membuat materi lebih mudah dipahami?"

Target:

**≥ 80% positive responses.**

Positive means users select:

* Very helpful
* Helpful

---

# 63.10 Course Quality

Every MVP course should receive learner feedback.

Target:

**Average course rating ≥ 4.2/5**

with at least:

**30 ratings per mature course**

before using the rating as a major quality signal.

Additional target:

**≥ 80% of learners say they would recommend the course to someone with the same learning goal.**

---

# 63.11 Retention

Retention is more important than raw registrations.

### Day 7

Target:

**≥ 35% learner retention**

### Day 30

Target:

**≥ 20% learner retention**

### Day 90

Target:

**≥ 15% learner retention**

Retention should be calculated from activated learners.

---

# 63.12 Learning Streak

Target:

**≥ 25% of activated learners establish a 7-day learning streak.**

This metric should remain secondary to actual learning outcomes.

SuperBright should avoid optimizing for streaks at the expense of meaningful learning.

---

# 63.13 Career Value

For career-oriented courses, ask learners whether the product helped them make measurable progress.

Within 90 days:

Target:

**≥ 30% of career-track learners complete a portfolio project.**

Target:

**≥ 20% create or substantially improve their CV/portfolio.**

Target:

**≥ 15% report applying for at least one relevant job, internship, freelance opportunity, or professional opportunity.**

These are behavioral outcomes, not guarantees of employment.

---

# 63.14 AI Assistant

If Bright AI is included in the MVP:

### Adoption

Target:

**≥ 30% of activated learners use Bright AI at least once.**

### Repeat Usage

Target:

**≥ 40% of Bright AI users use it more than once.**

### Helpfulness

After an AI interaction:

Target:

**≥ 4.0/5 average helpfulness score.**

### AI Safety / Quality

Target:

**< 2% of sampled AI responses require major correction due to materially incorrect educational information.**

Regulatory, legal, financial, or government-related answers should receive additional validation and source attribution.

# 63.15 Community

If community features are included:

Target:

**≥ 20% of activated learners interact with the community at least once.**

Target:

**≥ 50% of learner questions receive a response within 24 hours.**

Target:

**≥ 80% of reported harmful/spam content is reviewed within 24 hours.**

# 63.16 Conversion to Paid

The MVP should test willingness to pay.

Target:

**≥ 5% of activated free users make a paid transaction within 90 days.**

Paid transaction may include:

* Subscription
* Premium course
* Certificate
* Learning path
* Other validated premium product

A stronger signal:

**≥ 20% of users who reach a meaningful premium feature convert to paid.**

The second metric should be evaluated only among users who actually encounter a relevant paywall or paid offering.

# 63.17 Subscription Retention

If subscriptions are launched during MVP:

Target:

**≥ 60% of first-month subscribers remain subscribed into month two.**

Target:

**Monthly voluntary churn < 10%** after the initial trial period.

These should be measured separately from users who cancel because of failed payments.

# 63.18 Instructor Success

For the first 50–100 courses:

Target:

**≥ 80% of published courses meet SuperBright quality standards.**

Target:

**≥ 70% of instructors successfully publish a course without significant admin intervention.**

Target:

**≥ 30% of instructors publish a second course or update their first course within 90 days.**

This tests whether the instructor marketplace can become sustainable.

## Website

Target:

**≥ 99.5% uptime**

during MVP measurement.

## Page Performance

Target:

**LCP < 2.5 seconds** for key pages under representative mobile conditions.

Key pages:

* Homepage
* Course catalog
* Course detail
* Learning dashboard

## Video

Target:

**≥ 95% successful video playback sessions**

without a playback-related user-visible error.

# 63.20 Mobile Experience

Because a large proportion of Indonesian users access services through mobile devices, mobile experience should be a core MVP metric.

Target:

**≥ 60% of learning sessions occur successfully on mobile.**

Target:

**≥ 90% of mobile users can complete a lesson without encountering a critical UX issue.**

# 63.21 Customer Support

Target:

**≥ 80% of support tickets receive a first response within 24 hours.**

Target:

**≥ 90% of critical payment/account issues are resolved within 48 hours.**

Customer satisfaction target:

**≥ 4.0/5**

# 63.22 Business Sustainability Signal

The MVP does not need to be profitable.

It does need to demonstrate evidence that the economics could eventually work.

Track:

* Customer acquisition cost
* Average revenue per paying user
* Subscription conversion
* Course purchase conversion
* Gross margin
* Content production cost
* Support cost
* Infrastructure cost

Initial target:

**LTV/CAC ≥ 2**

with the longer-term objective of reaching:

**LTV/CAC ≥ 3**

The MVP should treat these as directional targets because early cohorts can produce noisy estimates.

# 63.24 MVP Launch Gates

SuperBright should not automatically move from MVP to large-scale expansion just because it reaches the registration target.

Expansion should require evidence across **three dimensions**.

## Gate A — Product Value

At least:

* ≥ 30% Successful Learners
* ≥ 40% project submission for project-based courses
* ≥ 4.2/5 course rating
* ≥ 80% positive Indonesia-context feedback

## Gate B — Retention

At least:

* ≥ 35% Day-7 retention
* ≥ 20% Day-30 retention
* ≥ 15% Day-90 retention

## Gate C — Commercial Signal

At least:

* ≥ 5% free-to-paid conversion
* Evidence of repeat purchases/subscriptions
* Positive contribution from at least one initial customer segment

If only one or two gates are achieved, SuperBright should iterate on the product rather than immediately expanding the course catalog.

---

# 63.25 MVP Kill / Pivot Signals

The following should trigger investigation rather than immediate scaling:

### Problem

High registrations but:

**<20% activation**

Potential issue:

* Onboarding
* Value proposition
* Course discovery

### Problem

High activation but:

**<20% course completion**

Potential issue:

* Content quality
* Course length
* Learning experience
* Motivation

### Problem

High completion but:

**low project completion**

Potential issue:

* Projects are too difficult
* Poor instructions
* Insufficient practice

### Problem

High engagement but:

**low willingness to pay**

Potential issue:

* Weak premium proposition
* Incorrect pricing
* Insufficient perceived value

### Problem

High satisfaction but:

**low retention**

Potential issue:

* Users only need one course
* Weak learning paths
* Weak recurring value

These signals should be investigated by cohort and user segment before making product decisions.

# 63.26 The Most Important MVP Question

At the end of the first 90 days, SuperBright should be able to answer:

> **"Apakah orang Indonesia bukan hanya mau belajar di SuperBright, tetapi benar-benar menjadi lebih capable setelah menggunakannya?"**

The strongest evidence would be:

**User discovers a goal → learns → practices → builds something → demonstrates a skill → improves their career/life capability → returns for the next goal.**

That loop should become the foundation for SuperBright's next product phase.
